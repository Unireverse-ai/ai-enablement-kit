# Internal champion model

A champion is the person inside the client's team who owns the workflow's operating routine after our engineers leave. We name the champion in stage 01, so that every stage after it is designed with one specific person in mind.

A champion is not a cheerleader, and not an extra IT support desk. The role is operational: they keep the workflow running, reviewed and changing in an orderly way.

## Selection

Choose a champion who meets these conditions. Check each against the workflow owner's view, not only the sponsor's.

| Criterion | Why |
|---|---|
| Works in the workflow, or next to it, most days | They notice problems first and are trusted by operators |
| Has time set aside, written into their plan | The role fails when it is added to a full workload |
| Is respected by the operators and reviewers | The role relies on persuasion, not authority |
| Can talk to IT and to the business | They carry questions in both directions |
| Is likely to remain in the role beyond the first year | Handover is wasted if the person moves on |
| Has a deputy | Absence and leaving are expected |

Avoid choosing the sponsor, who lacks time, or the most senior technical person, who may be unreachable for operators. A workflow owner can be the champion in a small team, but that merges two roles and is recorded as a decision.

Selection record: ⟦champion⟧ · ⟦deputy⟧ · ⟦time allocated⟧ · ⟦agreed by sponsor and the champion's manager, date⟧.

## Responsibilities

| Area | The champion |
|---|---|
| Routine | Runs the weekly check and monthly review from the [90-day plan](90-day-plan.md); keeps the [metrics](metrics.md) current |
| People | Answers operators' questions first; knows who is signed off; arranges training for new joiners using the [session plans](../enablement/session-plans/) |
| Feedback | Collects recurring corrections and requests from operators and brings them to the workflow owner as candidate changes |
| Documents | Keeps the [quick reference](../enablement/operator-quick-reference.md), runbook and sign-off record accurate |
| Escalation | Decides when something goes to the owner, the internal builder, IT & security or back to us; uses the pause route when needed |
| Review | Prepares the numbers for the sponsor at each checkpoint |

The champion does not approve changes to prompts, rules or models. The workflow owner does, through [change control](../governance/change-control.md). They do not own data protection decisions, which belong to IT & security.

## Handoff of ownership

Ownership passes in steps so that no day exists on which no one is responsible.

| Step | When | What passes | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Named | Stage 01 | The champion is named and joins the interviews | Selection record |
| 2. Shadowing | Stages 03–04 | The champion attends build reviews and labels eval cases; helps draft the runbook | Attendance and the runbook draft |
| 3. Co-running | Stage 05, day 0 to 30 | The champion runs the routine with an engineer present | Weekly checks held; engineer's notes |
| 4. Leading | Day 31 to 60 | The champion runs it alone; engineers review the change log | Monthly review held without engineers' preparation |
| 5. Owning | Day 61 onwards | The champion owns the routine; engineers attend the day 90 review only | Day 90 record |

A step is not complete until the champion and the lead engineer both say so and write the date. If a step is not complete on schedule, we say why and move the date; we do not skip the step.

## Champion changes

| Event | Response |
|---|---|
| Champion leaves or changes role | The deputy takes over at once. The workflow owner nominates a new deputy within ⟦agreed time⟧ and repeats steps 2 to 4 for them |
| Both champion and deputy unavailable | The workflow owner runs the routine and informs the sponsor |
| Champion cannot keep the time | The sponsor reduces the workload or names a different champion. The decision is recorded |

## Signs the model is working

Read these as prompts for a conversation, not as scores.

- Operators go to the champion first and are answered the same day.
- The monthly review happens when scheduled and the metrics are ready beforehand.
- Corrections have arrived at the workflow owner as proposals, not complaints.
- The quick reference was updated at least once since handover.

## Signs it is not

- The champion tells us they have no time.
- Operators have stopped logging corrections.
- Changes appear in the configuration without a change record.
- The monthly review has been skipped twice running.
